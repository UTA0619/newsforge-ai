How It Works

Automated in 3 simple steps

Set it up once. Get polished newsletters forever.

1

Connect your RSS sources

Add any RSS feed — TechCrunch, Hacker News, industry blogs, or custom sources. We poll them every 6 hours automatically, detecting new content the moment it publishes.

2

Claude AI summarises & curates

Our Claude-powered pipeline reads every article, writes a crisp 3-sentence summary, extracts the key takeaway, and scores relevance — entirely without human input.

3

Drafts land in Beehiiv

Polished newsletter drafts are pushed to your Beehiiv or Buttondown account automatically. Review, click send — done. Every article is logged in Airtable for full transparency.
