Accuracy & Trust

What if the AI gets it wrong?

Your reputation is on the line with every newsletter. Here is exactly how NewsForge protects your editorial integrity — and what happens when something looks off.

🔒

3-sentence format minimises hallucination

Shorter AI outputs have dramatically fewer factual errors. Our prompt constrains Claude to exactly 3 sentences per article — it summarises what’s there, not what it thinks should be there.

🔗

Every source is logged and linked

Every summary is paired with its source URL in Airtable. When you review a draft, you can click straight to the original article in one tap — verify in seconds, not minutes.

👁

Human review is mandatory — always

NewsForge never sends automatically. Every draft lands in Beehiiv as a draft, waiting for your eyes. You are the editor. If something looks wrong, you fix it or delete it before a single subscriber sees it.

🌟

Relevance scoring filters low-quality articles

Articles scoring below your threshold (default: 60/100) are automatically excluded. Bad sources produce lower scores and get filtered out over time without any manual work.
