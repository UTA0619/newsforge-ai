FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI summarisation actually work?

We use Anthropic's Claude API to process each article. Our custom prompt instructs Claude to write a 3-sentence summary, extract the single most important takeaway, and score the article's relevance. The results are stored in your Airtable base for full auditability.

Can I use my own RSS feeds and newsletter topics?

Absolutely. You can add any public RSS feed — from major publications to niche industry blogs. There's no limit on the number of sources. You can also configure topics and keywords to filter articles by relevance score.

Which newsletter platforms do you support?

We currently support Beehiiv and Buttondown out of the box. Additional platforms (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Ghost) are on the roadmap. The Make.com architecture makes it straightforward to add any platform with an API.

Do I need technical skills to set up NewsForge AI?

Basic familiarity with Make.com and Airtable is helpful, but we provide full setup documentation and onboarding support. Most customers are up and running within 2 hours of signing up.

What happens after my free trial ends?

At the end of your 7-day trial, you'll be automatically moved to the paid plan you selected. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends with no charge. You manage your subscription directly via the Stripe Customer Portal.
